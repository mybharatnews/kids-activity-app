📚 Grammar Learning

A, An, The, This, That, Is, Are shikho!

🔤 A vs An
"A" — consonant (k, g, b, c, d, ...) pehla vaparay
"An" — vowel (a, e, i, o, u) pehla vaparay
🍎
An apple
🐱
A cat
🐘
An elephant
⚽
A ball
👉 This vs That
"This" — najik ni vastu mate
"That" — dur ni vastu mate
📕
This is a book
🌳
That is a tree
✅ Is vs Are
"Is" — ek vastu mate (He is, She is, It is)
"Are" — ghana vastu mate (They are, We are)
👦
He is a boy
👧👦
They are kids
